# Avalokit

**An AI team that joins your org chart and understands your company more deeply the more it works.**

Avalokit is a free Windows app for building a team of AI employees, each hired into a role on your org chart and working alongside you and your colleagues. Each AI employee keeps its own memory and career, so it gets better at your work the more you use it, on the same AI model, without retraining. You use your own AI account and pay your AI provider directly; Avalokit adds no fee or markup.

**Launching December 2026.**

## Work you can hand off

Avalokit's AI employees don't stop at an answer. They carry the work through and hand you finished files:

- Market and competitor research reports, with sources
- Excel models with live formulas, and data analysis
- Presentations you can edit in PowerPoint, web pages and dashboards
- Images, video, narration and music
- New code and changes to existing projects, CAD drawings, 3D models and map analysis

Before handing anything over, the employee who made it looks at the actual rendered result and fixes what it sees.

## A team on your org chart

- **Hire by role.** There's no fixed catalog of agents to pick from. Hire for whatever role your company needs, from marketer to interior designer.
- **People and AI on one org chart.** Invite colleagues, and people and AI employees work in one company, following reporting lines and collaboration links. Several people can work with the same AI employee.
- **Solo or as a team.** AI employees delegate to each other, consult each other and meet to work through one agenda, each bringing its own expertise. When one employee can finish a task, it does it alone.
- **Big projects, carried to the finish.** Agree on completion criteria, stages, the points where people step in, and a budget in a completion charter. Once it's signed, the team carries the project through within that scope. Schedule recurring work, and it starts on time.
- **You set the permissions.** Each employee has a work scope and an approval threshold, so you decide which actions, such as sending things outside the app or system access, wait for your approval.

## Why it gets better with use

- **Memory** is what an AI employee has lived through at your company: instructions, feedback, meetings, the files it has read and the context of what it has made.
- **Career** is what it can take anywhere: values set at hiring, and specialties refined through real work.
- After its work, the employee uses outcomes and feedback, including failures, to refine its memory, expertise and working methods, without retraining the model. What worked stays; wrong judgments get corrected.
- Recurring work at your company becomes **frames**, and know-how for each tool builds up in the company's shared **tool manuals**. A path one employee finds, the others can use.
- All of this belongs to the employee, not the model. Switch models, and its memory and career stay.

## Works with your company's files and tools

- Reads the Word, PowerPoint, PDF and Excel files you upload, finds the passages it needs and cites them as evidence.
- Researches the web and works directly in signed-in web pages.
- Connects to the services your company already uses through their official APIs and MCP.
- Uses AI models from many providers through OpenRouter, and each employee can run on a different model.

## Your data stays on your computer

Your org chart, company files and AI employees' memory are all stored on your own PC or server. Avalokit doesn't collect your data: we keep no accounts on our servers, and the app sends us no usage logs. Only what the work needs leaves your computer, and it goes only to the AI model you choose and the tools your employees use. Turn on Remote access to reach your company from outside the office, or from a Mac, in a web browser.

## What you need

- A Windows PC or server: a personal PC, a company server or a cloud server you manage
- One OpenRouter key, for AI models from many providers
