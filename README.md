# Avalokit

Avalokit is a free Windows app for building a team of AI employees, each hired into a role on your org chart and working alongside you and your colleagues. Each AI employee keeps its own memory and career, so it gets better at your work the more you use it, on the same AI model, without retraining. You use your own AI account and pay your AI provider directly; Avalokit adds no fee or markup.

**Launching December 2026.**
