# Avalokit

**Your AI team. Growing with experience.**

Avalokit is a free Windows app for building a team of AI employees, each hired into a role on your org chart and working alongside you and your colleagues. Each AI employee keeps its own memory and career, so it gets better at your work the more you use it, on the same AI model, without retraining. You use your own AI account and pay your AI provider directly; Avalokit adds no fee or markup.

**Launching December 2026.** [Get notified at launch](#get-notified-at-launch)

## Built for real work

Avalokit is for people and teams who want AI to take on real work, not just answer prompts.

- **Hire into roles.** Add AI employees to your org chart the way you would bring on a new hire, in whatever roles your work needs.
- **Get finished work.** AI employees deliver finished files with built-in studios for Office documents, web pages, images, video, audio, code, data, maps and 3D. They also work with the services your company already uses, through web browsing, APIs and MCP.
- **Grow with experience.** Memory is what an AI employee has lived through at your company. Career is what it can take anywhere: its values and specialties. After each piece of work, it uses outcomes and feedback, including failures, to refine its memory, expertise and working methods. In our founder's daily use, that has meant better, more personalized results, less repeated explaining and fewer back-and-forths.
- **Work as a team.** AI employees delegate to and consult one another, and meet to tackle the same issue from their own roles. Invite colleagues to the org chart; several people can share the same AI employee.
- **Your data on your own machine.** Your company's data, including your AI employees' memory, is stored on your own PC or server. What AI employees send to your AI provider to do the work follows that provider's terms.

## What you need

- **A Windows host:** a personal PC, a company server or a cloud server you manage. From a Mac, you can use your company through a web browser connected to the Windows host.
- **An OpenRouter key:** one key gives access to AI models from many vendors. You pay OpenRouter directly at its rates.
