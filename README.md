# Avalokit

Avalokit is a free Windows app for building a team of AI employees, each hired into a role on your org chart and working alongside you and your colleagues. Each AI employee keeps its own memory and career, so it gets better at your work the more you use it, on the same AI model, without retraining. You use your own AI account and pay your AI provider directly; Avalokit adds no fee or markup.

**Launching December 2026.**

## Get notified at launch

To get an email when the first release is out, click **Watch** at the top of this page, choose **Custom**, tick **Releases**, then **Apply**. You need a GitHub account. You can also follow along at [avalokit.ai](https://avalokit.ai).

## Platform

Avalokit runs on Windows. The computer that hosts your company can be a personal PC, a company server or a cloud server you manage yourself.

There is no Mac app. From a Mac, you can use your company through a web browser connected to your Windows host.

## Cost

Avalokit is free. You connect your own OpenRouter key and pay your AI provider directly; Avalokit adds no fee or markup to AI usage.

## Your data

Your company's data, including your AI employees' memory, is stored on your own PC or server. What your AI employees send to an AI provider or a connected tool to do their work follows that provider's terms. See the [FAQ](https://avalokit.ai/faq) and the [Privacy Policy](https://avalokit.ai/privacy/).
